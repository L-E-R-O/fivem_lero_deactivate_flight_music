# ByeBye Flight Music 😴✈️

**Tired of that annoying cinematic flight music every time you enter a plane in FiveM?**  
*🎵 dun dun duuun duuun dun duuun duuun 🎵*  
Yeah… I feel you.

This tiny, standalone Lua script does **one thing only**:

**Disables the GTA flight/airplane interior music completely.**  
No more ear torture during long flights. Pure peace. 🥹

### Why people love it
- Stops the repetitive "flying cinematic track" instantly  
- Super lightweight
- Perfect for roleplay servers

So that this script can be found: 
- disable flight music FiveM
- no airplane music
- turn off plane interior music
- disable aircraft cinematic sound
- FiveM stop flying music
- no flight theme GTA V RP

Happy flying – and stay kind up there.

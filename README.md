## 💭 What is this?

Ever hopped into a plane in FiveM and been greeted by *that* cinematic music? You know the one...  
*🎵 dun dun duuun duuun dun duuun duuun 🎵*

Yeah, we've all been there. 😅

This lightweight script does **one simple thing**: it disables the GTA V flight music completely, so you can enjoy your flights in peace!

## ✨ Features

- 🎯 **One job, done well** – Stops the airplane interior music instantly
- 🪶 **Super lightweight** – Just a few lines of code, zero performance impact
- 🔌 **Plug & play** – Drop it in and forget about it
- 🎭 **Perfect for RP servers** – No more immersion-breaking cinematic tracks
- 🤝 **Works with everything** – Fully standalone, no dependencies

## 📦 Installation

1. **Download** this resource
2. **Drop** the folder into your server's `resources` directory
3. **Add** `ensure fivem_lero_deactivate_flight_music` to your `server.cfg`
4. **Restart** your server and enjoy the silence! ✨

That's it! No configuration needed. Just pure, peaceful flying. 🕊️

## 🤔 Why Use This?

- **For players**: No more repetitive music during long flights
- **For server owners**: Happy players = better experience
- **For immersion**: Let your custom radio or ambient sounds shine
